# SOUL.md — Operator voice template (for founder-brand work)

This file is THE most important file for the founder-brand pack. The
whole point is: the skill should sound like YOU, not Jay, not ChatGPT.

## My voice in 3 sentences

<3 sentences describing how you actually write. Not "professional yet
approachable" — specific. E.g. "I write declarative, with one claim
per sentence. I lead with the receipt before the explanation. I don't
soften with hedges; I either claim it or cut it.">

## Phrases I use a lot

<5-10 phrases that show up in your writing repeatedly. Voice
fingerprints. E.g.:>

- "The lever is..."
- "What actually moved the number..."
- "Most teams blame X; the real issue is Y..."
- "Here's the receipt:"
- "Specifically:"

## Phrases I refuse

<5-10 phrases you'd never write. Banned in addition to the framework's
banned-patterns list:>

- "Synergy"
- "Leverage" (as verb)
- "Circle back"
- "In today's fast-paced world"
- "Game-changing"
- "Delve into"
- "Navigate the landscape"

## My posting voice settings

| Dimension | My setting |
|---|---|
| Formality | Casual / Professional / Direct |
| Sentence length | Short (≤15 words) / Medium / Mixed |
| Confidence | High (claims outright) / Hedged / Calibrated |
| Humor | Dry, occasional / Never / Often |
| Profanity | Never / Sparingly / Often |
| First-person | I / We / Both |
| Emoji | Never / Sparingly (≤1/post) / Often |

## What 'good' looks like for me

<2-3 sentences describing what a great post of yours feels like when

## How the founder-brand skill uses this

Every post the skill generates:
1. Uses your phrases-I-use list (frequency, not forced)
2. Refuses your phrases-I-refuse list
3. Pulls receipts from your stories reservoir
4. Respects your voice settings (formality / length / confidence / etc.)
5. Stays inside your won't-write boundaries
6. Aims at your "good looks like" bar

The JMC voice rubric (specificity, one claim per post, format for
skim, no engagement bait) is enforced regardless. SOUL.md layers YOUR
fingerprint on top.
